Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Overview

Image Size Picker is a Chrome extension that displays image dimensions, original resolution and file size on web pages. We do not collect personal user data or send browsing history to the developer.

On-page processing

When you enable measurement in a tab, the extension identifies image regions and reads their dimensions in your browser. Image URLs and measurement results may be cached temporarily in memory to avoid repeating requests.

To determine original resolution or file size, the extension may request an image or information about it from the server hosting that image. These requests go directly from your browser to the image's source server. They are not sent to a server operated by the developer.

Session storage

Version 1.1.0 uses chrome.storage.session to keep track of which tabs have measurement enabled during the current browser session. This state is not synced between devices. Reloading or navigating a tab resets its measurement mode.

The extension does not store a browsing history, visited-site list, image collection or personal profile.

Permissions

  • storage — keep measurement mode state for each tab during the browser session.
  • activeTab — interact with the tab where you activate the extension.
  • <all_urls> — detect and measure images on websites and frames, including images hosted on a different domain or CDN.

Analytics and third-party services

The extension does not include analytics, advertising networks or data collection services. Websites and image source servers may apply their own privacy policies to requests made to them.

Policy updates

If the extension's data handling changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
